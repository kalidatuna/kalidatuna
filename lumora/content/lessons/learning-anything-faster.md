AI might be the best learning tool ever made, as long as you use it as a **tutor**, not a shortcut. Here's how to learn faster and remember more.

## Explanations at exactly your level

The same topic can be explained a dozen ways. Ask for the one that clicks for you.

```prompt
Explain how mortgages work. Start with a one-paragraph explanation for a complete beginner using an everyday analogy. Then list the 5 terms I'll hear most often, each in one sentence. Then ask me a question to check I understood.
```

Useful dials: "for a 12-year-old", "using a cooking analogy", "assume I know basic maths but nothing about finance", "with a diagram described in words".

## The Feynman loop

The most effective study technique is explaining something in your own words. AI makes it effortless:

1. Read or watch something about a topic.
2. Write your own explanation, rough and imperfect.
3. Ask the AI to find the gaps.

```prompt
I'm learning about [topic]. Here's my explanation in my own words: [your explanation]. What did I get right, what's missing or wrong, and what's one question that would test whether I really understand it?
```

## Quiz yourself (retrieval practice)

Testing yourself beats rereading. Ask for quizzes, one question at a time:

```prompt
Quiz me on [topic] with 10 questions, one at a time. Mix multiple choice and short answer. Wait for my answer, tell me if I'm right, explain briefly, then move on. Get harder if I'm doing well.
```

## A study plan that fits your week

```prompt
I want to learn [skill] well enough to [goal] in 8 weeks. I can study 30 minutes on weekdays and 1 hour on Saturdays. Create a week-by-week plan with free resources, a small practice project each week, and a checkpoint to test myself.
```

## Language learning superpowers

- **Conversation practice:** "Let's chat in beginner Spanish about my weekend. Correct my mistakes gently after each message."
- **Voice mode:** Many apps let you talk out loud, which is great for pronunciation and confidence.
- **Real-life phrases:** "Give me 15 phrases I'll need at a pharmacy in Italy, with pronunciation."

> [!TIP]
> Turn on voice mode during a walk and ask the AI to teach you about something you're curious about. It's like a podcast that answers your questions.

## Learn from documents and videos

Upload a PDF or paste a transcript, then: "Summarise the key ideas, then quiz me on them." Perfect for manuals, course readings and long articles.

## Key takeaways

- Ask for explanations at your level, with analogies and check-in questions.
- Use the Feynman loop and quizzes; active recall beats rereading.
- Build a realistic study plan around your actual schedule.
