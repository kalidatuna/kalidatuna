Some of the most valuable uses of AI are also the most sensitive: understanding a doctor's letter, a confusing bill, a tenancy agreement or a tax form. Used well, AI makes you a far better-prepared patient, customer and citizen. Used badly, it can mislead you. Here's the safe way.

## The golden rule: understand, prepare, confirm

1. **Understand:** use AI to explain jargon and structure.
2. **Prepare:** use AI to organise your questions and information.
3. **Confirm:** take decisions with a qualified professional or an official source.

## Health: a better-prepared patient

AI can help you:

- Translate medical terms in a letter or test result into plain English.
- Prepare a clear timeline of symptoms before an appointment.
- Write down the questions you want to ask, so you don't forget them in the room.

```prompt
I have a GP appointment about recurring headaches. Help me prepare. Ask me questions to build a short timeline (when they started, frequency, triggers, what I've tried). Then produce a one-page summary and the 5 most useful questions I could ask the doctor.
```

> [!WARNING]
> AI must never replace urgent care. Chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding or thoughts of self-harm need emergency services or a crisis line **immediately**, not a chatbot.

## Money: decode bills and decisions

```prompt
Here's the text of my energy bill with account numbers removed: [paste]. Explain each charge in plain English, tell me what's normal versus unusual, and list questions I could ask the supplier to lower my bill.
```

Other good uses: understanding pension or investment jargon, comparing loan offers (ask for total cost over the term), and checking the maths on a budget. For personal investment, tax or debt decisions, use AI to *prepare* and then talk to a regulated adviser or official service.

## Legal: understand before you sign

AI is excellent at turning dense contracts into plain language:

```prompt
Here's a section of my tenancy agreement: [paste]. Summarise what it means in plain English. Highlight anything unusual, one-sided or that I should ask about before signing. Note: I'm in [country/state].
```

Laws vary by country and change over time, so always mention where you live, and confirm anything important with a lawyer, an advice service, or official government guidance.

## Protect your privacy

Before you paste a document:

- Remove names, addresses, account numbers, dates of birth and ID numbers.
- Use a temporary/incognito chat for sensitive topics if your app offers one.
- Check whether your chats are used for training (Settings → Data controls).

## Key takeaways

- Understand, prepare, confirm. AI explains; professionals decide.
- Emergencies go to emergency services, never to a chatbot.
- Redact personal details and mention your location for anything legal or financial.
