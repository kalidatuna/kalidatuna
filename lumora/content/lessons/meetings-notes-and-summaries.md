Meetings are where work time disappears. AI can't make your meetings shorter (sadly), but it can make the before and after almost effortless.

> [!NOTE]
> **Work rule #1:** check your employer's AI policy. Many companies provide approved tools (like Microsoft Copilot or Gemini for Workspace) and forbid pasting confidential information into personal accounts. Following the policy protects you.

## Before: prepare in five minutes

```prompt
I have a 30-minute meeting with [who] about [topic]. My goal is [outcome]. Here's the background: [notes/emails]. Give me: a 3-point agenda, the 3 questions I most need answered, likely objections and how to respond, and a one-sentence opening.
```

## During: let the tools take notes

Most video-call platforms (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet) and many standalone apps can now record, transcribe and summarise meetings. Always:

- **Tell everyone** it's being recorded or transcribed (in many places this is a legal requirement).
- Check where the recording is stored and who can see it.

## After: from transcript to action

A raw transcript is long and messy. Ask for a structure you'll actually use:

```prompt
Here's the transcript of our meeting: [paste]. Produce:
1. A 3-sentence summary.
2. Decisions made.
3. Action items as a table: task, owner, deadline.
4. Open questions and unresolved disagreements.
5. A short follow-up email I can send to attendees.
Only include things actually said, and mark anything you're unsure about with (?).
```

That last line matters: it reduces made-up action items.

## Turn messy notes into clarity

Photograph your handwritten notes or paste your scribbles:

```prompt
These are my rough notes from a call: [paste]. Clean them up into a clear summary with headings. Don't add facts that aren't in the notes.
```

## Weekly time-savers

- **Monday:** "Here's my calendar and to-do list. What should I prioritise, and what can I decline or delegate?"
- **Friday:** "Turn these bullet points into a short weekly update for my manager."
- **Long threads:** "Summarise this email thread: where did we land, and what's still needed from me?"

## Try it now

Take your notes from your most recent meeting (remove anything confidential) and run the after-meeting prompt. Send the follow-up email. You'll look impressively organised.

## Key takeaways

- Follow your workplace AI policy and use approved tools.
- Prep with goals, questions and objections; summarise with decisions, owners and deadlines.
- Ask the AI to flag uncertainty and stick to what was actually said.
