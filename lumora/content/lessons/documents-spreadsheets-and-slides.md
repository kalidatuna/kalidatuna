Office work is mostly three things: documents, spreadsheets and slides. AI now lives inside all three (Microsoft Copilot in Word/Excel/PowerPoint, Gemini in Google Docs/Sheets/Slides) and any standalone assistant can help too.

## Documents: from blank page to solid draft

The trick is to give the AI your raw material, not ask it to invent from nothing.

```prompt
I need a one-page proposal to my manager for a 4-day trial of flexible Fridays for our team. Here are my bullet points: [paste]. Audience: a busy, data-minded manager. Structure: problem, proposal, benefits, risks + mitigations, how we'd measure success. Keep it under 400 words.
```

Then improve it with the critique pattern: *"What would a sceptical manager push back on? Strengthen those parts."*

## Spreadsheets: formulas without tears

Describe what you want in plain English:

```prompt
In Google Sheets, column A has dates, column B has sales amounts, and column C has region names. Write a formula that totals sales for the "North" region in March 2026 only. Explain how it works in one sentence.
```

AI can also explain a formula you've inherited: "What does this formula do, step by step?"

> [!WARNING]
> Always **test formulas on a few rows where you already know the answer.** A formula that looks right can silently be wrong, and spreadsheet errors spread.

## Data analysis: ask questions of your data

Most paid assistants can analyse uploaded spreadsheets (CSV/Excel) by writing and running code behind the scenes.

```prompt
Here's a CSV of our monthly sales for two years. Find the 3 most important trends, any unusual months, and create a simple chart. Explain the findings for a non-technical audience and tell me how confident you are in each.
```

Remove personal or confidential data first, and sanity-check totals against your own numbers.

## Slides: outline first, design second

```prompt
I have 10 minutes to present our Q3 results to the whole company (non-experts). Key message: we grew 18% but customer complaints rose. Create a 7-slide outline: title, one key point per slide, a suggested visual, and speaker notes of 2–3 sentences each.
```

Then use the built-in AI in PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva or Gamma to turn the outline into designed slides. Edit for accuracy and remove filler.

## A healthy workflow

1. **You** decide the goal, audience and key points.
2. **AI** drafts structure and words.
3. **You** check facts, numbers and tone.
4. **AI** polishes: shorter, clearer, consistent formatting.
5. **You** own the final version.

## Key takeaways

- Feed AI your raw material; don't ask it to invent your content.
- Test every formula on known cases.
- Outline slides first, then design. Keep facts and numbers under your control.
