AI isn't just a text box anymore. It can **see** through your camera, **talk** naturally, and increasingly **act** in your web browser. Together, these turn your phone into a genuinely helpful companion for everyday life.

## Vision: "what is this?"

Point your camera or upload a photo and ask. Brilliant everyday uses:

- **Appliances and gadgets:** "What does this blinking light on my boiler mean?"
- **Plants and gardens:** "What's wrong with this tomato plant's leaves?"
- **Menus and labels abroad:** "Translate this menu and flag anything with nuts."
- **Documents:** "Summarise this letter and tell me if I need to do anything."
- **DIY:** "Which screw do I need for this bracket?" (with a photo of the bracket and your screw box)
- **Learning:** "Explain this diagram from my textbook."

> [!NOTE]
> Vision is impressive but not perfect. For safety-critical things (gas, electrics, medication, allergies), confirm with the manufacturer, a professional or the original label.

## Live voice + camera

Some apps support live video conversations: you show the AI what you're looking at and talk it through in real time. Try it while cooking, fixing something, or exploring a new city.

## Browser agents

AI agents can now operate a web browser: clicking, typing, scrolling and navigating, to complete tasks for you. This might be an "agent mode" in your assistant, an AI-powered browser, or a browser extension.

**Good tasks for browser agents:**
- Research and compare: "Find 3 well-reviewed dentists near me that accept new patients, with their prices for a check-up."
- Fill in tedious forms (you approve before submitting).
- Collect information across many pages into a table.
- Routine online admin you'd otherwise postpone.

**Rules for using them safely:**

1. Stay nearby for important steps; approve submissions and payments yourself.
2. Log in yourself when needed. Don't type passwords into the chat.
3. Be wary of letting agents loose on unfamiliar sites; pages can contain hidden instructions designed to hijack them (prompt injection).
4. Check results: agents can misread pages or quietly skip steps.

## Putting it together

A real example of all three:

> You photograph a broken dishwasher part → vision identifies the part number → you ask an agent to find it in stock at three retailers with prices and delivery times → you choose and pay yourself.

That's a 45-minute errand done in five.

## Key takeaways

- Vision turns your camera into an instant explainer; confirm anything safety-critical.
- Voice + camera makes hands-on tasks easier.
- Browser agents handle online errands; supervise, log in yourself, approve payments.
