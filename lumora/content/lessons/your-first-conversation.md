Most people open an AI app, type "hello", get a bland answer and conclude it's not for them. Let's do better. In fifteen minutes you'll have an assistant that knows how you like to work.

## Minute 0–3: Install and sign in

- Install the **mobile app** *and* bookmark the **web version** on your computer. You'll use both.
- Sign in with an account you control. If it's for work, follow your employer's rules about which account and tool to use.

## Minute 3–6: Check privacy settings

Find **Settings → Data controls / Privacy** (wording varies). Decide:

- **Should your chats be used to improve the model?** You can usually opt out.
- **Is "memory" on?** Memory lets the assistant remember facts across chats. Useful, but review what it saves now and then.
- **Temporary / incognito chats** are handy for one-off sensitive questions.

> [!WARNING]
> Never paste passwords, full card numbers, or other people's private information into any AI chat, whatever your settings.

## Minute 6–11: Write your custom instructions

Most assistants let you add standing instructions (called *custom instructions*, *personalisation*, or *preferences*). This is the single biggest quick upgrade. Copy and adapt this:

```prompt
About me: I'm a [job/role] living in [country]. I'm comfortable with technology but new to AI. I mostly want help with [e.g. emails, planning family life, learning Spanish, running my bakery].

How I like answers: Plain English. Short first, details on request. Use bullet points for steps. Use [metric/imperial] units and [currency]. If something is uncertain or might be outdated, say so. If my request is unclear, ask me one question before answering.
```

## Minute 11–15: A first conversation that matters

Pick something real that's on your mind this week. Here's a template:

```prompt
Here's something I need to sort out this week: [describe it in 2–3 sentences, including any deadline and what "done" looks like].

1. Ask me up to 3 questions to understand my situation.
2. Then give me a simple plan with the first step I can do in 10 minutes.
```

Answer its questions honestly. Then follow up, which is where the magic is:

- "Make step 2 simpler."
- "Write the message for step 1 for me."
- "What could go wrong, and how do I avoid it?"

## Good chat hygiene

- **One topic per chat.** Start a new chat when you change subjects, and give it a clear name.
- **Correct it.** "That's not right, the deadline is Friday" works perfectly.
- **Save winners.** When a prompt works brilliantly, save it. (Our Prompt Library and bookmarks make this easy.)

## Key takeaways

- Check privacy and memory settings before you start.
- Custom instructions turn a generic assistant into *your* assistant.
- Start with a real problem, let the AI ask questions, then keep refining with follow-ups.
