Your voice is the fastest way to use AI, and AI audio tools have become remarkably good. This lesson covers talking *to* AI and creating audio *with* AI.

## Voice mode: talk instead of type

Most assistant apps have a voice mode where you have a natural spoken conversation. It's ideal for:

- **Thinking out loud:** "Help me think through whether to take this job." Talking is faster than typing and often more honest.
- **Hands-busy moments:** cooking, driving (hands-free), walking.
- **Language practice:** real conversations with gentle corrections.
- **Rehearsing:** interviews, presentations, difficult conversations.

> [!TIP]
> Try a "walk and talk": put your earphones in, start voice mode, and say "Interview me about my goals for the next three months, one question at a time."

## Transcription: never take notes again

AI transcription turns recordings into text with remarkable accuracy. Uses:

- Voice memos → clean notes: "Turn this rambling voice memo into a tidy to-do list."
- Interviews and lectures → searchable text and summaries.
- Meetings (with everyone's consent) → minutes and action items.

Many phones and note apps now transcribe built-in; assistants can transcribe uploaded audio too.

## Voiceovers and text-to-speech

Text-to-speech tools (like ElevenLabs, or features in Canva, CapCut and others) produce natural voiceovers for:

- Explainer videos and presentations
- Turning articles into audio you can listen to
- Accessibility: reading text aloud for people with low vision or dyslexia

## Music generation

Tools like Suno and Udio can create songs, complete with vocals, from a text prompt: a birthday song with your friend's name, a jingle for a small business, background music for a video.

```prompt
An upbeat acoustic folk song about my friend Sam's 40th birthday, mentioning his love of terrible puns and long hikes. Warm, singalong chorus.
```

Check each tool's licence before using music commercially.

## The ethics of voice cloning

Some tools can clone a voice from a short sample. Powerful, and easily misused.

- **Only clone voices with clear, informed consent** (including your own, for your own projects).
- **Never use clones to deceive** (pretending to be someone in calls or messages).
- **Protect your own voice:** be mindful about posting long clean voice recordings publicly, and set up a family "safe word" (see the Safety module).

## Key takeaways

- Voice mode is ideal for thinking, practising and hands-busy moments.
- Transcription turns any audio into notes, summaries and action items.
- Voiceovers and music are easy to create; respect licences and consent, always.
