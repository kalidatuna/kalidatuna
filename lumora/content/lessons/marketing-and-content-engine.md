Marketing needs a steady stream of content, which is exactly what drains small teams. AI can multiply your output, but only if you feed it real substance. Generic in, generic out.

## The raw material only you have

Before you prompt, gather your "gold":

- **Real customer questions** (from emails, DMs, reviews).
- **Stories:** behind-the-scenes moments, mistakes, wins, customer transformations.
- **Opinions:** what you believe about your industry that others don't.
- **Specifics:** numbers, names (with permission), places, prices.

## One idea → a week of content

```prompt
Here's a real story from our business this week: [story]. Our audience: [who]. Brand voice: [3 adjectives + example sentence].
Turn it into:
1. An Instagram caption (hook in first line, under 120 words)
2. A LinkedIn post (story → lesson → question)
3. A newsletter section (150 words)
4. Three short video ideas with hooks
5. A Google Business Profile update
Keep every piece specific to the story. No generic marketing clichés.
```

## Your brand voice guide

Create this once and reuse it everywhere (ideally as a skill or custom assistant):

```prompt
Interview me to create a brand voice guide for my business. Ask about our customers, values, personality, words we love and words we hate. Then write a one-page guide with do/don't examples I can give to any AI tool or new staff member.
```

## Product descriptions and ads

- Give AI your product facts *and* the customer's problem: "Who's it for, what problem does it solve, what makes it different?"
- Ask for variations to test: "5 headlines: benefit-led, curiosity, social proof, urgency, playful."
- Check every claim. AI can invent features, awards or guarantees you don't offer.

## A simple weekly rhythm

| Day | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Collect stories and questions from last week | 15 min |
| Tuesday | AI drafts the week's content from them | 20 min |
| Wednesday | Edit, add photos, schedule | 30 min |
| Friday | Check what performed; tell the AI what worked | 10 min |

> [!WARNING]
> Platforms and readers are getting better at spotting bland AI content. Your edge is specificity and honesty. Edit until it sounds like a human who knows their stuff.

## Key takeaways

- Feed AI real stories, questions, opinions and specifics.
- Repurpose one idea into many formats with a single prompt.
- Build a brand voice guide, check every claim, and run a simple weekly rhythm.
