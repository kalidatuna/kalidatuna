You've covered the foundations, prompting, everyday life, work, skills, automation, creativity, business, safety and power-user tools. That's more than most people will ever learn about AI. Now let's make it **stick**, because knowledge without habits fades within weeks.

## The 90-day structure

### Days 1–30: Daily habit

Goal: reach for AI automatically.

- Use AI for **one real task every day**. Small is fine.
- Keep a **wins list**: what you did and the time saved.
- Save every prompt that works well into your library.

### Days 31–60: Build your systems

Goal: stop repeating yourself.

- Write your **custom instructions** properly (revisit the Foundations module).
- Build **two skills or custom assistants** for your most frequent tasks.
- Set up **one automation** and **one scheduled task**.

### Days 61–90: Level up

Goal: tackle something ambitious.

- Pick one **meaningful project**: a side project, a big work improvement, a vibe-coded tool, a learning goal.
- Use **deep research** and **reasoning** modes to plan it.
- Teach someone else what you've learned. Teaching locks it in.

## Create your personal plan

```prompt
I've just completed a course on practical AI. Here's my situation: [job, family, goals, time available]. The tasks that drain me most: [list]. Things I'd love to achieve this year: [list].
Create my personal 90-day AI plan:
- Days 1–30: a daily micro-habit and 10 specific tasks to try
- Days 31–60: the 2 skills/assistants and 1 automation I should build, with first steps
- Days 61–90: one ambitious project, broken into weekly milestones
Include a simple weekly check-in question.
```

## Keeping up without the overwhelm

AI news moves at breakneck speed. You don't need to follow it all:

- **Follow one or two trusted sources**, not twenty.
- **Check your main assistant's "what's new" page** once a month.
- **Re-try a task every few months** that AI used to struggle with. Capabilities change quickly.
- **Ignore the hype cycle.** Ask: "Does this help with something I actually do?"

## Your wins list is your proof

After 90 days, your wins list becomes powerful evidence, for a promotion conversation, a CV, a client pitch, or just your own confidence. "I automated our enquiry handling and saved 5 hours a week" is a sentence that opens doors.

> Mark the date 90 days from now in your calendar with the reminder "Review AI plan + wins list." Future you will be glad.
