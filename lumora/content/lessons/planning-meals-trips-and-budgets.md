Planning is invisible work that eats hours every week. AI is superb at it because planning is mostly juggling constraints, and you can hand all the constraints over at once.

## Weekly meal plans that fit real life

The secret is constraints. The more you give, the more usable the plan.

```prompt
Plan 5 weeknight dinners for 2 adults and 2 kids (ages 6 and 9, one doesn't like spicy food). Budget about $90 for the week. Max 30 minutes active cooking. We have a slow cooker. Use overlapping ingredients to reduce waste. Then give me one shopping list grouped by supermarket aisle.
```

Follow-ups that work well:

- "Swap Wednesday for something vegetarian."
- "Make Friday a fakeaway pizza night."
- "Turn the leftovers from Monday into Tuesday's lunch."

> [!TIP]
> Snap a photo of your fridge or cupboard and ask, "What can I make tonight with this in 25 minutes?" Most assistants can read images.

## Trip itineraries without 20 open tabs

```prompt
I'm planning 4 days in Lisbon in May with my partner. We like food, walking, viewpoints and one museum max. Not into nightlife. Mid-range budget. Make a day-by-day plan with morning/afternoon/evening, grouping things that are close together. Flag anything that usually needs booking ahead.
```

Then refine: "Add a rainy-day backup for each day." "Which day is best for a day trip to Sintra?"

> [!WARNING]
> Opening hours, prices and transport details change. Use AI for the *shape* of the trip, then confirm key details on official websites (or use an assistant with web search and check its links).

## A budget you'll actually understand

You don't need to paste your bank details. Use categories and rough numbers:

```prompt
Help me build a simple monthly budget. Take-home pay: about $3,400. Fixed costs: rent $1,300, car $280, phone $45, insurance $120. I want to save $300/month and I have $2,000 of credit card debt. Suggest category amounts using a simple method, explain it in plain English, and give me 3 realistic ways to free up $150 a month.
```

## Other planning wins

- **Moving house:** a week-by-week checklist from 8 weeks out.
- **Parties:** timeline, shopping list and a rainy-day plan.
- **Cleaning:** a rotating schedule split fairly across the household.
- **Big purchases:** a comparison table of options with total cost of ownership.

## Try it now

Use the meal plan prompt with your own household details. Ask for the shopping list. Time how long it takes compared to your usual Sunday planning.

## Key takeaways

- Constraints (people, budget, time, preferences) make plans realistic.
- Use photos of your fridge, documents and tickets as input.
- Let AI shape the plan; confirm time-sensitive details at the source.
