Video and slides used to take days. AI collapses the slow parts (scripting, rough cuts, captions, layouts) so you can focus on the story.

## The story comes first

Whether it's a 30-second social clip or a 20-minute presentation, start with structure:

```prompt
I want to make a 60-second video explaining [topic] to [audience]. Goal: [what they should do or feel afterwards]. Write a script with a hook in the first 3 seconds, 3 key points, and a clear call to action. Include on-screen text suggestions and B-roll ideas for each section.
```

## AI video tools, by job

| Job | What AI can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Generate clips** | Create short video clips from text or an image (e.g. Sora, Veo, Runway, Kling) |
| **Edit** | Remove filler words and silences, auto-cut, reframe for vertical video (CapCut, Descript, Premiere) |
| **Captions** | Accurate automatic subtitles in many languages |
| **Avatars** | Presenter-style videos from a script (e.g. Synthesia, HeyGen), for training videos and explainers |
| **Clean audio** | Remove background noise and echo |

> [!NOTE]
> Generated video quality improves monthly. It's excellent for short clips, backgrounds and concepts; for anything long or brand-critical, mix generated footage with real footage and careful editing.

## Edit by editing text

Tools like Descript turn your video into a transcript. Delete a sentence in the text and it's cut from the video. Remove every "um" with one click. It's the most beginner-friendly way to edit talking-head videos.

## Presentations in minutes

1. **Outline with your assistant:** audience, goal, key message, time limit (see the Work module's prompt).
2. **Generate a first deck:** PowerPoint (Copilot), Google Slides (Gemini), Canva, or Gamma can turn an outline into designed slides.
3. **Edit ruthlessly:** one idea per slide, fewer words, real data, your own examples.
4. **Rehearse with AI:** paste your speaker notes and ask, "Where will the audience get lost or bored?"

```prompt
Here are my slides and speaker notes: [paste]. I have 10 minutes. Time each section, tell me where I'm over-explaining, and suggest a stronger opening line and closing line.
```

## Disclosure and trust

If you use AI-generated people, voices or realistic scenes, be open about it, especially in marketing or anything news-like. Many platforms now require labels on realistic AI content.

## Key takeaways

- Script and structure first; tools second.
- Use AI for generation, editing, captions and clean audio; edit by text where possible.
- Outline → generate → edit → rehearse for presentations. Label realistic AI content.
