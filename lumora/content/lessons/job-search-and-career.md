Job hunting is exhausting: dozens of applications, each needing tailoring. AI can take on much of the grind while you focus on the human parts.

## Tailor your CV to each role

```prompt
Here's my CV: [paste]. Here's the job description: [paste]. 
1. Which of my real experiences best match the job's top 5 requirements?
2. Rewrite my summary and the 4 most relevant bullet points to highlight those matches, using strong verbs and numbers where I have them.
3. What gaps would a hiring manager notice, and how could I honestly address them?
Do not invent any experience, skills or numbers.
```

That final line is essential. Exaggerations get found out in interviews.

## Cover letters that sound human

Generic AI cover letters are easy to spot. Make yours specific:

```prompt
Write a cover letter for this role [paste JD]. Use these real details: why I'm interested [your reason], a specific story where I [achievement], and something specific I admire about the company [detail]. Tone: confident and warm, not formal. Max 250 words. Avoid clichés like "I am writing to express my interest".
```

## Interview practice

Voice mode makes this especially powerful: practice out loud.

```prompt
Act as an interviewer for [role] at [type of company]. Ask me 8 questions one at a time, mixing behavioural ("tell me about a time…") and role-specific questions. After each answer, give brief feedback using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) and a stronger version of my answer.
```

## Salary and negotiation prep

- "What's a typical salary range for [role] in [city], and what factors affect it?" (Use web search and verify with salary sites.)
- "Help me write a polite counter-offer asking for [amount], with reasons based on [your strengths]."
- "Role-play the negotiation. You're the hiring manager and you push back."

## Plan your career with AI

```prompt
I'm a [current role] with [X years] experience. I enjoy [things] and dislike [things]. I'm curious about moving into [field]. Suggest 3 realistic career paths, the skills gap for each, how AI is likely to change each role, and a 90-day plan to test the most promising one.
```

> [!TIP]
> AI skills themselves are now a strong CV asset. List concrete examples like "built an automated weekly report that saved 3 hours/week", not just "familiar with AI".

## Key takeaways

- Tailor every application by matching real experience to the job's needs, never inventing.
- Give AI specific stories and details so letters sound like you.
- Practise interviews out loud with feedback, and prepare salary negotiations in advance.
