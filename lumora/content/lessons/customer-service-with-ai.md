Customers want fast, accurate, friendly answers. AI can deliver the first two at any hour, and help you deliver the third, if it's set up carefully.

## Level 1: AI-drafted replies (human sends)

The safest start. AI drafts; you review and send.

```prompt
You help me reply to customer messages for [business]. Use ONLY the information in our business brain below. If the answer isn't there, say "I'll check and get back to you" rather than guessing. Tone: [tone]. Keep replies under 100 words.

Business brain: [policies, prices, hours, FAQs]

Customer message: [paste]
```

This alone often cuts reply time by more than half.

## Level 2: An FAQ assistant on your website

Many website builders, help desks and chat tools now offer AI assistants trained on your own content. Set them up so they:

- Answer **only from your approved information** (this is often called *grounding*).
- **Hand off to a human** for refunds, complaints, account changes, anything sensitive or anything unclear.
- Make it clear to customers they're talking to an AI assistant, and how to reach a person.
- Collect contact details for follow-up when they can't help.

## Responding to reviews

```prompt
Here's a customer review: [paste]. Write a reply as the owner. If positive: thank them specifically, mention a detail they raised, under 50 words. If negative: acknowledge, apologise for their experience without admitting legal fault, offer to make it right offline with contact details, under 80 words. No defensiveness.
```

## Turn conversations into improvements

Once a month:

```prompt
Here are last month's customer messages (personal details removed): [paste]. What are the top 5 recurring questions or complaints? For each, suggest a fix: a website FAQ, a policy change, a product change or a template reply.
```

This is customer research you'd otherwise never have time for.

> [!WARNING]
> Never let an AI make commitments it can't keep: refunds, discounts, delivery dates or legal statements. Businesses can be held to what their chatbots promise. Keep those decisions with humans.

## Measure the right things

- Time to first reply
- Percentage resolved without escalation
- Customer satisfaction (a simple thumbs up/down)
- Errors caught in review (should fall over time)

## Key takeaways

- Start with AI-drafted, human-sent replies grounded in your business brain.
- FAQ assistants must answer from approved info and hand off clearly to humans.
- Use monthly AI analysis of messages to fix problems at the source.
