AI image tools let anyone create illustrations, mock-ups, invitations, product shots and art from a text description. Most major assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) can now create and edit images directly in the chat, alongside dedicated tools like Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Canva and Ideogram.

## The image prompt formula

Describe what a photographer or illustrator would need to know:

**Subject + Setting + Style + Lighting + Mood + Composition**

```prompt
A cosy reading nook by a rainy window (subject + setting), soft watercolour illustration (style), warm lamp light against blue-grey rain (lighting), calm and nostalgic (mood), wide shot with a sleeping cat on the cushion (composition).
```

## Style words that work

- **Medium:** photograph, watercolour, oil painting, pencil sketch, 3D render, flat vector illustration, paper cut-out, pixel art.
- **Lighting:** golden hour, studio lighting, overcast, neon, candlelight.
- **Camera (for photos):** close-up, wide angle, top-down, shallow depth of field.
- **Mood:** playful, serene, dramatic, minimal, retro.

## Editing images with words

Modern assistants can edit an existing image:

- "Remove the person in the background."
- "Change the wall colour to sage green."
- "Make this product photo look like it's on a marble countertop."
- "Turn this photo of my dog into a children's book illustration."

> [!TIP]
> Iterate one change at a time. "Same image, but make the sky sunset orange" works better than rewriting the whole prompt.

## Text in images

AI has become much better at writing words inside images (posters, invitations, logos), but always check spelling carefully, and for anything official (business signage, printed invitations) do a final pass in a design tool like Canva.

## Practical uses

- **Home:** party invitations, birthday cards, colouring pages for kids, room redesign ideas from a photo.
- **Work:** blog headers, presentation visuals, social posts, product mock-ups, mood boards.
- **Small business:** menu illustrations, seasonal promos, simple ad concepts.

## Keep it consistent

For a series (like a set of social posts), describe a fixed style and reuse it:

```prompt
Style guide for all images in this chat: flat vector illustration, limited palette of navy, coral and cream, rounded shapes, lots of white space, no text. First image: a person watering plants on a balcony.
```

> [!NOTE]
> Rules on commercial use, copyright and disclosure vary by tool and country, and are still evolving. Check each tool's terms before using images commercially, and avoid generating real people's likenesses without consent.

## Key takeaways

- Prompt formula: subject, setting, style, lighting, mood, composition.
- Edit iteratively with one change at a time.
- Keep a reusable style description for consistent series; check text and terms of use.
