## Send a useful project brief

Copy these prompts into an email:

```text
Project or business:
Website or repository link:
What needs to change:
What a successful result looks like:
Target deadline:
Budget range, if set:
```

Use screenshots, public links, or synthetic sample data for the first discussion. Keep passwords, API keys, and customer records out of your initial email.

## What we agree before work starts

- The pages, features, or specific fix included in the project.
- The deliverables, acceptance checks, price, and timeline.
- Required content, access, hosting, and external service costs.
- The review process and any follow-up support included.

## What you receive

The agreed code or files, setup and usage instructions, a summary of verification, and any known limitations. Hosting, integrations, production deployment, and ongoing support depend on the agreed scope.
